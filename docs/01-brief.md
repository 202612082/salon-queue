# Salon Queue Management System — Project Brief

## 1. Project Overview

The Salon Queue Management System is a web-based application designed to simplify and digitize customer queue management in salons.

It allows customers to join a queue using their mobile phones, select services and preferred barbers, and track their waiting status. Salon staff can manage customers, control the queue, and update service progress through dedicated dashboards.

The main goal is to reduce unnecessary waiting, improve customer experience, and make salon operations more organized and efficient.

## 2. Problem Statement

Traditional salons often manage customer queues manually, which can lead to long waiting times, confusion about customer turns, and poor communication between customers and staff.

Customers frequently have no information about their queue position or estimated waiting time.

This system aims to solve these problems through a simple digital queue management platform.

## 3. Target Users

1. **Customers:** Join the queue, select services and barbers, and track their turn.
2. **Receptionist:** Manage the waiting list, add walk-in customers, and call the next customer.
3. **Barbers:** View assigned customers, start and complete services, and update availability.
4. **Salon Owner/Admin:** Manage services, prices, staff availability, queue settings, and daily statistics.

## 4. Must-Have Features

1. **Digital Queue Joining:** Customers can join the queue through their mobile phones by entering their details and selecting services.
2. **Barber Selection:** Customers can choose a preferred barber or select the fastest available barber.
3. **Live Queue Tracking:** Customers can view their token number, queue position, and estimated waiting time.
4. **Reception Dashboard:** Receptionists can add walk-in customers, call the next customer, cancel entries, and mark no-shows.
5. **Barber Dashboard:** Barbers can view their current customer, start services, mark services as completed, and update availability.
6. **Waiting Area Display:** A TV-friendly screen displays currently served customers and the next five queue tokens.
7. **Owner Administration:** Owners can manage services, prices, barbers, queue availability, and daily statistics.
8. **Bilingual Interface:** The application supports English and Gujarati languages.

## 5. Features Not Included

The following features are outside the scope of this project:

- Online payments and payment gateway integration.
- Advance bookings for future dates.
- SMS and WhatsApp notifications.
- Loyalty and reward programs.
- Multiple salon branch management.

## 6. Look and Feel

The application will have a premium, modern salon aesthetic with a dark charcoal background (#1F2937), elegant gold accents (#D4A017), clean layouts, rounded cards, and a mobile-first responsive design.

The interface will support both English and Gujarati languages and will be designed to work across mobile phones, tablets, and TV displays.

## 7. Project Objective

To develop a user-friendly and responsive Salon Queue Management System that improves customer convenience, reduces manual queue management, and helps salon staff operate more efficiently.
